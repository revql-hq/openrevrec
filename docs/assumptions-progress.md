# Forms assumptions review: implementation status

This tracks the September 22 screen-by-screen review. "Addressed" means the specific observed failure has a working path and regression coverage where accounting behavior changed. "Partial" means the remaining boundary is still real; a warning or a label is not accounting support.

| Finding | Status | Current boundary or next work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Account mapping | Partial | Four required journal roles are flow types, not a four-account limit: reusable profiles, contract overrides, and obligation overrides can route to multiple GL codes. A contract profile supplies balance and billing dimensions; obligation profiles can independently route revenue accounts and dimensions. The journal stays balanced overall, and dimension-level imbalance is flagged for close review. A reviewed list of exact approved account-dimension combinations checks journal lines. Invalid listed combinations block close; the accountant can choose whether uncovered accounts require review or also block close when the list is declared complete. This local preflight does not simulate ERP account structures, date-effective rules, or posting. Interunit entries remain unsupported. Because billing is recorded against the contract and its asset or liability is presented at the contract level, obligation-level balance routes would need an explicit, reviewed billing allocation and a reconciliation back to the contract balance; the existing obligation revenue profile cannot supply that answer. |
| 2. Mapping transitions | Partial | Opening balance transfer, external reconciliation, and historical-account runoff are explicit. Runoff carries each contract asset or deferred balance by account and dimensions across months; the accountant records a dated closing allocation with a rationale, and missing allocations block close. The Journal and workbook label provisional allocations as draft. A later transfer can move every retained old route. A zero-balance route starts using the new mapping directly. External transfers and recorded runoff splits still require reconciliation to the actual ledger; the app does not infer the release order or reconstruct pre-cutover account history. |
| 3. Workspace setup | Partial | Desktop setup asks for company, reporting currency, account-effective month, and defaults. Contract entry now states the workspace currency and lack of FX calculation; monetary activity and opening-position forms label the currency beside entered amounts. Contract-level monthly cutover is available; fiscal calendar, FX, and parallel books remain outside scope. |
| 4. Opening positions | Partial | Home filters by active service terms or outstanding position. A contract can enter a reconciled monthly opening position through its form or workbook; pre-cutover periods are excluded, and a declared cutover requires the opening before further activity. An accepted opening can be corrected with a reason and complete replacement facts; later activity replays, closed periods require reopening, and the workbook shows the current opening with its correction link. A per-period source manifest can identify omitted active contracts and compare current-month invoice amounts, externally priced usage values, each contract's cutover opening balances, and mapped obligation-level recognized amounts and cumulative measures when independently supplied. Source obligation rows can retain their legacy references beside mapped accounting IDs for audit. These checks catch offsetting errors across contracts or obligations that aggregate GL controls miss. A multi-obligation opening without source obligation rows, or a measure-driven opening without its source measure, stays under review. Pre-cutover transaction completeness, the independent register's completeness, mapping from legacy obligations, and complex historical modifications still need review. |
| 5. Customer identity | Addressed | Customer choice is deliberate; source-system references are unique within their source. |
| 6. Contract defaults | Partial | Prices/SSP are blank, template is deliberate, annual end is inclusive and correct. Cancellable and evergreen contracts record an assessed accounting end, rationale, trigger, and optional review date. A named reviewer can document an unchanged conclusion and schedule the next review without a financial amendment; a changed assessment still requires a dated amendment. An indefinite term, automatic renewal, and automatic reassessment remain unsupported. |
| 7. Allocation exceptions | Partial | Variable, usage, and credit components can target current obligations with a rationale and exact reconciliation. A variable or usage amount can target one month within one time-based series obligation. Prospective amendments retain earned targeted revenue and allocate each component's remaining amount to unsatisfied targets, with the component trace visible in reports. Later estimate changes to an unchanged pre-modification variable or usage promise follow its original allocation, with an already-delivered share caught up in the change month. Revised consideration for already satisfied targets, an initial legacy opening with period targeting, targeted prospective treatment after unallocated opening or adjustment amounts, and ambiguous original-promise or satisfaction-path changes still need separate treatment. |
| 8. Recognition entry | Addressed | Exact days, equal touched months, and normalized calendar-month proration are distinct choices with entry previews; activity measures show prior/proposed values. |
| 9. Material rights | Partial | Exercise before later time-based or point-in-time delivery is recorded separately from satisfaction; unexercised rights still recognize at expiry. An exercised right can be linked to one same-customer renewal contract whose date range covers the right's delivery window and whose obligations stay inside it, with a confirmed new-consideration-only price. The renewal contract may start before delivery or end afterward. Contract view and export reconcile the old right allocation with the new price and monthly revenue without adding it twice. Asset/deferred balances remain separate and any offsetting position needs presentation review; renewal obligations outside the right's delivery window, amended renewals, and changing an exercised right remain unsupported. |
| 10. Usage | Partial | Finite-unit usage remains available. A separate right-to-invoice path supports one uncapped service when the accountant documents why invoice value corresponds directly to value delivered. A dated flat-rate change retains the earlier unit price for earlier delivery. Alternatively, actual delivered units can carry a reviewed amount from an external pricing source, with a required reference; this supports externally priced tier or overage amounts without calculating their rules or posting billing. An independent monthly source list compares record IDs, units, and amounts; ID-only lists remain under review. Each month's values are summed and rounded once; no future units are forecast. Minimum commitments, automatic tier/overage pricing, non-calendar billing periods, mixed obligations, opening positions, and other amendments remain unsupported. |
| 11. Corrections | Addressed | Source facts can be corrected without rewriting history, including imported facts. Negative billing credits link to a workspace invoice or an external original invoice reference. |
| 12. Balance classification | Partial | Journal screen labels contract asset as simplified revenue less billing. Unconditional receivables and cash-before-invoice events remain outside the model. |
| 13. Modifications and combinations | Partial | Treatment is deliberate, empty-obligation termination is allowed, and date edits are guarded. A qualifying distinct fixed-price addition can be linked as a separate contract, with separate revenue and balances and an offsetting-balance presentation warning. If repricing the original makes the amendment's net increase fail the separate-contract test, one prospective modification handles all-distinct remaining service. A mixed fixed-price amendment can assign a reviewed lifetime amount and catch-up, prospective, or retained treatment to each obligation; a progress-method catch-up can also record a revised completion percentage when scope changes. The calculation preserves prior revenue, posts the resulting catch-up, and reconciles to the revised price. It requires every original obligation, no earlier opening or accounting change, and no later accounting amendment. A paragraph 17 combination conclusion can record source agreements for the primary customer or reviewed related-party customers in one accounting contract, one schedule and balance, and source-specific billing and usage for close reconciliation. Each source retains its legal customer ID; a different customer needs a documented relationship rationale. Combined revenue and balances appear under the primary customer, so a legal-customer split still requires separate review. The accountant records the criterion and dates and verifies the relationship; the app does not infer timing or corporate control. Changed recognition methods for catch-up obligations, variable-price mixed modifications, more complex modification chains, historical conversion, and later combination revisions remain unsupported. |
| 14. Temporal contract views | Addressed | Effective terms are the default, baseline remains available, and change detail shows before/after terms. |
| 15. Financial preview | Addressed | Preview focuses the effective period and shows affected periods, obligations, and journal differences. |
| 16. Revenue screen | Addressed | Unscheduled revenue is visible; horizon and customer filters limit the matrix; calendar-month scope is explicit. |
| 17. Scenarios | Partial | Comparison lists proposals and accounting detail. Rebase ignores descriptive edits and combines distinct identified positive invoices, a separately identified credit against an older workspace or external invoice with a new positive invoice, or two identified credits against different earlier invoices. For workspace originals, their source invoice identities must also be distinct. Two positive-billing corrections can rebase when they target different original invoices and every cross-branch original and replacement source identity is distinct; replay and close validation still apply. Separately identified usage source rows and corrections to different identified usage rows can also rebase after cross-branch identity checks and full accounting replay. Cumulative progress or milestone facts, including corrections, can rebase when they target different obligations on the same contract; full replay and close validation still apply. Duplicate source identities, credits to the same or duplicate-source original invoice, a new invoice whose reference matches a credit's original, corrections to the same invoice or usage row, replacement-identity collisions, and progress changes to the same obligation still conflict. Unreferenced invoices, unidentified credits or usage facts, credits with incomparable original identity types, rate corrections, contract treatment changes, and account-policy changes remain subject to conflict review. More complex accounting conflicts remain unsupported. |
| 18. Journal handoff | Addressed | Posting guide, account detail, stable batch ID, and user-entered external journal references are available. A revised close now has a balanced replacement delta against each recorded posted checkpoint in the journal screen and workbook. The accountant must confirm which batch is in the ledger; the app does not post or verify it. |
| 19. Close controls | Addressed | Independent billing and GL totals, optional close cutoff, scenario relevance, period-linked tasks, and accepted exceptions are in place. A separate source contract, invoice, priced-usage, and cutover-opening manifest compares identities, signed invoice amounts, priced-usage units and values, per-contract opening balances, and supplied obligation-level recognized amounts and cumulative measures. ID-only invoice or usage rows, older manifests without opening balances, multi-obligation openings without source splits, and measure-driven openings without source measures stay under review where applicable. The Source population screen groups all exception types by count in bounded panels; accountants can load more rows and open each actionable record or focus its source list. The workbook retains the full rows. Missing, unexpected, duplicate, unidentified, or mismatched records appear in close and workbook support. The accountant remains responsible for the independent extract, obligation mapping, and population filters. |
| 20. Close enforcement | Addressed | Blocking checks prevent close and every review item needs a recorded reason. Prior-period catch-up policy remains a separate design question. |
| 21. Import acceptance | Addressed | Read-only preview, atomic commit, source identity, duplicate detection, and stale-preview guard are in place. |
| 22. Import usability | Addressed | Structured amendment/correction sheets, command ordering, and downloadable row errors are in place. |
| 23. Change explanation | Addressed | Term comparisons, correction links, and broader reference search are available. |
| 24. Evidence | Addressed | Existing files can be reused and a ZIP includes the workbook and indexed files. A named reviewer records a conclusion, basis, and supported or exception disposition for each judgment change; file presence alone never clears close review. The recorded conclusion remains the accountant's responsibility. |
| 25. Dashboard attention | Addressed | A compact warning count opens the focused Accounting warnings report, and a separate count opens outstanding close checks; Home no longer says nothing needs attention while close review has open items. Calculation warnings carry source contract and obligation IDs into close review and export, so identically worded warnings on different contracts remain separate, count separately, show distinct contract IDs, and navigate to the right records. The close review uses the same actionable warning rows. A linked-balance warning can open both contracts. Tasks retain their direct completion and contract actions. |
| 26. Main-panel footer | Addressed | The status tray occupies the bottom row of the main panel, outside the scrolling content area, and remains at the bottom while the panel content scrolls. |

The [pilot boundary cases](accounting-boundary-cases.md) specify the examples and expected outputs to collect before extending the remaining calculations. The next accounting-model work should address ambiguous original-promise changes, more complex mixed modifications, linked-contract balance presentation, and more complex metered pricing. Legacy population completeness and historical amendments also require a migration review even when opening balances reconcile. Until then, the entry screens must keep these boundaries visible. [AASB 15 paragraphs 21, 73, 84–90, B16, and B39–B43](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) distinguish prospective treatment, the relative-SSP default, criteria for allocating variable consideration to particular obligations or services in a series, the invoice-value expedient, and accounting for option rights; the product captures the accountant's conclusion rather than inferring it from a price label.
