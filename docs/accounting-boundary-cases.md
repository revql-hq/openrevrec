# Accounting boundary cases for pilot review

The [screen-by-screen status](assumptions-progress.md) distinguishes supported behavior from partial support. These examples identify the facts a pilot accountant should bring before the remaining calculation paths are extended. They are acceptance cases, not instructions to force the facts into today's forms.

| Screen or workflow | Case to test with a real contract | Required product result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings / account mapping | One contract has implementation and subscription revenue in different GL accounts and different department or project dimensions. | Reusable profiles and separate obligation revenue accounts are supported when one dimension set applies to the whole contract. For different dimensions within the same contract, agree how its net balance and balancing entries are segmented before claiming an ERP-ready import. |
| Settings / mapping change | A deferred account changes while existing contracts have balances, and the controller wants old balances to run off in the old account. | Preserve each account's opening and monthly movement through runoff. The current transfer/external choices do not implement this policy. |
| Workspace setup | A contract is denominated in another currency, or reporting uses a 4-4-5 calendar or multiple accounting books. | Do not convert by changing the workspace currency or relabeling calendar months. Decide the rate, remeasurement, fiscal period, and book policy before adding inputs. |
| Contract entry | A cancellable or evergreen arrangement has no fixed accounting end date. | Record the assessed enforceable end, rationale, and reassessment trigger; revise service dates through a reviewed amendment when that assessment changes. Do not substitute an arbitrary far-future end date or assume renewal automatically. |
| Allocation | A bonus applies only to one service period in a multi-period series, or a specifically allocated component remains after a prospective modification. | A reviewed variable or usage amount can target one month of one time-based series obligation. For example, $1,200 base consideration over January–December plus a $120 June-only bonus yields $100 in each ordinary month and $220 in June; a $30 increase first included in July adds $30 to July as a catch-up. A prospective amendment keeps the $120 earned in June and allocates a revised base fee to the remaining distinct months. A completed June bonus cannot be prospectively repriced; original-promise variable changes after modification and initial legacy period targeting still need separate treatment. Test these conclusions against a real contract and memo. |
| Material rights | A renewal option is exercised in June for service delivered July–December, with a new renewal price or contract. | The right's existing allocation can now follow later delivery; test how the separate renewal price and resulting contract are linked and reconciled without double counting. Recording June exercise as June delivery would be misleading. |
| Usage | A customer owes a rate per unit with no contracted unit cap, possibly with tiers and overages. | Derive consideration from supported usage and pricing terms, then apply the documented allocation judgment. The current finite-unit obligation cannot represent this contract. |
| Billing / balances | An invoice creates an unconditional receivable before service, or cash arrives before an invoice. | Keep receivable, cash, and contract asset or liability distinct, with a specified external billing and posting workflow. Today's contract asset is simplified revenue less billing. |
| Modifications | An amendment adds a distinct service at its standalone price while discounting the remaining original service. | Support the accountant's mixed treatment and show separate and prospective effects, rather than choosing one treatment for the entire amendment. |
| Journal handoff | A closed batch was posted, then reopened and revised. | The journal screen and workbook now compare each recorded posted checkpoint with the revised batch at account and dimension detail and produce a balanced delta. Confirm which prior batch represents the ledger, particularly if several posting references exist; the app cannot verify the actual ledger. |
| Cutover / close | A legacy workbook omits a contract or source invoice but its totals still happen to reconcile. | Supply the independent contract register and current-month invoice/credit identities in Source population; investigate missing, unexpected, duplicate, or unidentified rows even when money totals match. This does not reconstruct pre-cutover invoice history or verify the extract's own completeness. |
| Corrections / close | A September invoice error is discovered after September closes, and policy calls for an October catch-up rather than a September restatement. | Capture economic and posting periods separately only after the policy for recognition, rollforward, comparative reports, and ledger export is specified. Current behavior requires explicit reopen for a change to a closed period. |
| Evidence | A signed agreement is linked but does not support the selected treatment, or a documented internal memo does. | A named reviewer now records the conclusion, support memo, disposition, and exception reason independently of file presence. The recorded review remains a human accounting judgment. |

For a pilot, collect one anonymized contract and its approved accounting memo, legacy schedule, invoices or usage source, chart-of-accounts mapping, and posted journal for each applicable row. Write the expected obligation allocation, monthly revenue, contract balance, and journal lines before changing the calculator. A case that cannot be stated at that level remains an explicit product boundary.
